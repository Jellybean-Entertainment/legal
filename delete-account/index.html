Loot Match! Sort & Match 3D – Delete Your Data

Last updated: October 16, 2026

You can ask Jellybean Entertainment Co., Ltd. to delete your Loot Match! Sort & Match 3D account and the game data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Find your Player ID (it is shown in the game's settings).
  2. Email privacy@jellybeanent.com with the subject Delete my data.
  3. Put your Player ID in the message.

We may contact you to confirm that the request is yours.

What we delete

Within 30 days of your request, we delete your account and the game data linked to it, including your player ID, progress, inventory and settings, and the analytics and diagnostic data tied to your player ID.

Once deleted, your progress and any purchased items cannot be restored.

What we may keep

  • Purchase records, where tax, accounting or other laws require us to keep them
  • Information we need to prevent fraud or resolve disputes
  • Copies in backups, which are deleted within 90 days
  • Aggregated data that no longer identifies you

Uninstalling the game

Uninstalling Loot Match! only removes the data stored on your device. Your account and game data stay on our servers until you ask us to delete them as described above.
