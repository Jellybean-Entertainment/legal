What to include in your message

  • Your Player ID: from the main menu, open Settings and tap Copy beside your Player ID. Paste it into your email.
  • Your device model: for example, iPhone 15 or Pixel 8.
  • Your OS version: your iOS or Android version.
  • The app version, if available.
  • What happened: what you were doing when the problem started, any error message, and screenshots or a screen recording if helpful.
  • For purchase problems: the item you bought, the purchase date and your Apple or Google receipt showing the order or transaction ID.

If the game won't open or you can't find a detail, send us what you can. You don't need all of this information to ask for help.

Please don't send passwords or full payment card details. You can hide unrelated personal information on receipts and screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my Player ID?

From the main menu, open Settings. Your Player ID is shown there. Tap Copy, then paste it into your email.

I bought Coins but didn't receive them

Check your internet connection, then close and reopen the game. If you've been charged and the Coins are still missing, contact us with your Player ID and purchase receipt. We'll investigate.

How do I get a refund?

  • App Store: sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com and choose “Request a refund” from the “I'd like to” menu. See Apple's refund instructions for the full steps.
  • Google Play: follow Google Play's refund instructions. You can also contact us with your order ID for help with a Google Play purchase.

Refund eligibility depends on the applicable store policy and your country or region.

My progress is missing

Loot Match! uses a guest account linked to your Player ID. Deleting the app, clearing its data or switching devices may cause you to lose access to that account or progress saved on your device.

Contact us with your current Player ID and, if you have it, your previous Player ID. Keep a copy of your Player ID and avoid uninstalling the game or clearing its data while we look into the issue.

The game crashes or won't load

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Close the game completely and open it again.
  • Check the App Store or Google Play for an update to Loot Match!
  • Restart your device and check that it has free storage space.

If the problem continues, contact us with your device model, OS version and what happened. Include your Player ID and app version if you can access them. Please don't uninstall the game or clear its data as a troubleshooting step, as you may lose access to your progress.

How do Lives work?

Lives are shown by the heart icon. Starting a new attempt uses one Life, and Lives refill over time. When you're below the maximum, the main menu shows how long until your next Life.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the instructions on our Delete Your Account & Data page. You can also open that page from the main menu by choosing Settings > Delete Account.

Once your account is deleted, your progress and purchased items cannot be restored. Uninstalling the game does not delete your account.
